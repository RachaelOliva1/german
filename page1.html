Places to visit

  1. Rothenburg ob der Tauber and The Romantic Road. Offering a classic fairy-tale experience featuring preserved medieval architecture, scenic countryside drives, and historic walled towns. Key highlights include the complete medieval fortifications of Rothenburg, the 350-kilometer scenic driving route, and iconic regional food and culture.
  2. Neuschwanstein Castle, Southern Bavaria. It is worth visiting for its iconic fairytale architecture, stunning Alpine setting, and rich royal history. Opinions on the experience are mixed, as some travelers note the heavy crowds and long travel times from Munich
  3. Lake Königssee. A stunning destination because of its emerald-green waters, towering limestone cliffs, and the iconic Pilgrimage Church of St. Bartholomew.
  4. Bavarian Forest National Park. A premier destination because it features Germany's oldest untamed wilderness, expansive trail networks, and unique wildlife viewing
