Germany's Biggest Tradition

Oktoberfest is the world's largest folk and beer festival, featuring massive tents, carnival rides, and traditional Bavarian food. Held annually in Munich, Germany, the 16-to-18-day event draws over six million visitors each year to celebrate local culture. Oktoberfest means a celebration of Bavarian heritage, a massive economic engine, and a global symbol of community tradition. For Germans, and particularly Bavarians, it represents local pride, cultural continuity, and the "fifth season" of communal gathering
